Example: when the kettle switches on

  1. P1 changes by more than 500 W.
  2. Effective gain immediately changes from 0.33 to 0.25.
  3. It stays at 0.25 for 0.1 seconds.
  4. It then starts ramping toward 0.33.
  5. If grid power crosses the 0 W target, gain drops to 0.15.
  6. It ramps from 0.15 back to 0.33 over 1 second.
  7. The slow bias remains frozen for 30 seconds.

A new step over the threshold restarts this sequence.

What to adjust

Less export overshoot:

  • Reduce Step gain from 0.25 to 0.22.
  • Reduce Brake gain from 0.15 to 0.10-0.12.
  • Increase Ramp time from 1 s toward 15 s.

Faster initial response:

  • Increase Step gain, but no higher than 0.30.
  • Reduce Hold time or Ramp time.

False step detections: increase Step threshold to 700-1000 W.

Missed appliance changes: reduce it to 300-400 W.

Steady export after settling: increase Target import, for example from 0 W to 50 W, then allow the slow bias several minutes to adjust.
